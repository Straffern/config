# Agent skill fit audit

## Recommendation

Adopt four pstack capabilities now: [`blast-radius`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/blast-radius/SKILL.md) for executable downstream-risk proof, [`principle-boundary-discipline`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/principle-boundary-discipline/SKILL.md) for parse-once edges, [`principle-encode-lessons-in-structure`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/principle-encode-lessons-in-structure/SKILL.md) for turning repeated corrections into enforcement, and [`why`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/main/pstack/skills/why/SKILL.md) for evidence-rated historical intent. Port the leaf behavior into OMP; do not import Cursor runners wholesale.

The net-new pstack shortlist is `principle-encode-lessons-in-structure`, `principle-boundary-discipline`, `blast-radius`, `why`, `principle-make-operations-idempotent`, `interrogate`, `principle-build-the-lever`, conditional `create-verification-skill` when a repository lacks a reusable real-surface harness, and conditional `show-me-your-work` for long unattended work where its evidence trail justifies the ceremony. These are independent choices, not a score bundle. `maintain-verification-skill` is companion-only after a verification skill exists. Use `principle-guard-the-context-window` for bulky delegation and `arena` only for consequential one-way-door designs.

Use these already-installed Matt skills deliberately at their real trigger: `diagnosing-bugs`, `tdd`, `code-review`, `codebase-design`, `domain-modeling`, `research`, `grilling`, and `writing-for-agents`. If adding new Matt catalog skills, start with `to-tickets` and `triage`, but only where their durable issue-tracker workflow is welcome. Do not install wrappers for capabilities already present.

Skip exact duplicates of current contracts (`principle-fix-root-causes`, pstack `tdd`, proof/clean-cutover/subtraction principles), broad routers (`Poteto Mode`, `ask-matt`), and host-specific Git/Cursor/Claude setup. The workflow is Jujutsu-first, so Git-index, branch, worktree, hook, or Claude-background-agent assumptions require a compelling leaf behavior and an explicit port.

## Corpus and method

This is a privacy-preserving fit audit of two different evidence sources. Their units are intentionally **not added together**:

- **OMP prompt ledger:** `~/.omp/agent/history.db` (`history`), with 11,340 prompt records, 4,085 distinct normalized prompt strings, 307 tagged sessions, and 57 working-directory values from 2026-03-20 through the live snapshot on 2026-08-20. Exact repetition and replay are substantial, so raw records indicate interaction load while normalized strings better approximate breadth.
- **Pi transcripts and tools:** `~/.pi/agent/sessions/` plus `~/.pi/agent/run-history.jsonl`, with 169 transcript-bearing parent sessions plus 71 metadata-only parents, 179 stored child transcripts, and 174 overlapping run-history rows from 2026-02-18 through 2026-08-17. Intent rates use 1,383 direct parent messages after excluding 56 injected skill payloads. Children are supervisor assignments and run history overlaps child activity.

Both audits used overlapping regex/keyword indicators plus qualitative inspection of their local stores. OMP is broader; Pi adds richer tool and outcome evidence. Rankings emphasize cross-corpus agreement, breadth, observed friction, existing capability, setup cost, ecosystem fit, and dependable incremental value—not summed activity. The stable profile is repository-first engineering: inspect, reason about design or root cause, constrain scope, implement/migrate, exercise real behavior, review/correct, then commit or continue. Nix is primary: 2,392 OMP records, 2,279 `.nix` references, and 4,172 `nix` commands. Elixir/Phoenix dominates application work: 7,924 `.ex`/`.exs` references and 2,516 `mix` commands. Ash is unknown; TypeScript (201 references) and GraphQL (79) are secondary. Jujutsu precedes Git, with 3,034 command matches versus 596.

Catalog sources: [pstack live skills](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack/skills), audited at [`cursor/plugins@51a96e0`](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/commit/51a96e0dd838404da19ba83dc70aa21eef71f868): 44 first-level skills, 44 `SKILL.md` files, and 77 bundled/support blobs (121 total), classified as 3 setup, 5 meta, 24 principle, and 12 operational. [Matt Pocock live skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills), audited at [`mattpocock/skills@0ab1b63`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/0ab1b63a410a03d3627979a109c8695de27af954/skills): 35 skills—18 engineering, 7 productivity, 4 misc, and 6 in-progress; `deprecated` is empty. Skill links below point to current first-party `main` files. Catalog and corpus snapshot date: **2026-08-20**. Review reconciliation left no unresolved score conflicts.

## Scoring

- **Fit — integer in [0,5]:** recurrence or relevance of the problem. `0` is absent/incompatible; `1` plausible but unobserved; `2` occasional/supporting; `3` recurrent in one corpus or broadly relevant; `4` strong cross-corpus recurrence; `5` dominant, cross-cutting, or repeatedly high-cost.
- **Benefit — integer in [0,5]:** dependable incremental improvement over current behavior and installed skills. `0` is incompatible or duplicate; `1` convenience/near-duplicate; `2` small or narrow improvement; `3` clear missing support; `4` large low-overlap improvement; `5` transformative with no adequate substitute.
- **Priority:** `Fit × Benefit`. Tables sort by Priority, then Fit, Benefit, and exact skill name. Beta, setup, orchestration, Git-only, and host-specific costs reduce Benefit rather than inflating Fit.

## Ecosystem and overlap cautions

- **Installed capability is not additive benefit.** Standards/Spec review, causal diagnosis, deep-module design, domain modeling, prototyping, primary-source research, merge-conflict help, TDD, wizards, grilling, and agent-document writing already exist. Thin routers, orchestration wrappers, and compositions must not be summed; they inherit problem Fit but usually only 0–1 Benefit.
- **Relationships are narrow, not universal prerequisites.** Pstack principle pairings are affinities; `Poteto Mode` is optional and only `figure-it-out` explicitly consumes its Principles section. Pstack `teach` uses `how` and/or `why` by scope; both `teach` and `technical-writing` consume `unslop`. Matt `ask-matt` routes promoted stable engineering and productivity skills only. The dependencies referenced by `implement`, `triage`, and `wayfinder` are present. Grilling wrappers are compositions, not independent capability. `diagnosing-bugs` reruns the original reproduction loop rather than requiring an invented full-suite pass. All `setup-matt-pocock-skills` outputs are under `docs/agents/`.
- **Do not bundle overlapping gains.** `why` reconstructs historical intent; `handoff` carries session state; recall/reflect retain memory; encode-lessons changes structure. Likewise, `interrogate` deduplicates findings, `blast-radius` proves downstream safety, and Matt `code-review` separates Standards from Spec. Each earns only its distinct output.
- **Host assumptions matter.** Cursor/Claude-native behavior, Git index/branches/worktrees, Graphite, tracker/gist ceremony, polling, and unbounded fan-out reduce Benefit. Borrow a valuable leaf behavior rather than importing a runner.
- **Ecosystem-specific skills stay narrow.** The measured Nix/Elixir, TypeScript/GraphQL, and jj/Git evidence is stated once in the method above. Course scaffolds, Husky, and shoehorn migrations have no recurring evidence. Beta status is not an automatic zero, but maintenance and disruptive setup cap dependable Benefit.

## Limitations

- OMP history was live during analysis, contains uneven replay/duplication, and lacks session IDs before June. Normalized equality both under-collapses paraphrases and over-collapses genuinely repeated terse decisions.
- Pi has 71 metadata-only parents and redacted run-history tasks with unavailable intent; child transcripts and run history overlap and are not additive. Its August-heavy reviewer campaign inflates some activity, and shell/path heuristics can overlap.
- Regex families overlap and qualitative samples remain uncertain. Keyword categories are indicators, not exclusive task labels. Neither corpus provides task-level cross-corpus deduplication, so no combined task count or percentage is defensible.
- Confidence is high for inventories, workload mix, and tool counts; medium-high for stable cross-corpus dimensions; medium for overlapping keyword intent and corrections; and low/unknown for Ash, standalone JavaScript, conflict resolution, human-only provisioning, prototypes, and catalog-specific setup triggers.
- Scores assess expected incremental value at the stated catalog commits and current harness. Upstream `main` links may evolve; re-audit source behavior before installing a runner or repository-wide setup skill.
- Privacy constraints intentionally omit raw prompts, source, secrets, credentials, identifiers, and proprietary details. Reasons therefore use aggregate evidence and generalized workflow descriptions.
